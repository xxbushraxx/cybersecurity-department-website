Department History

The Cybersecurity Department was established to meet the increasing demand for cybersecurity professionals and to address the growing challenges of protecting digital systems, networks, and information. The department focuses on preparing students with the knowledge and practical skills required to defend against cyber threats and support the development of secure digital environments.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality cybersecurity education, develop students' technical and analytical skills, and prepare qualified professionals who can contribute to protecting information systems, networks, and digital environments from evolving cyber threats.
