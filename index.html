what is cybersecurity?

Cyber security is the practice of protecting computers, networks, programs, and data from digital attacks, damage, or unauthorized access.

Core principles

  • Confidentiality: Keeping private data secret from people who should not see it.
  • Integrity: Making sure data is accurate and safe from unauthorized changes.
  • Availability: Ensuring systems and data are ready and open for use when needed.

common types of cybersecurity

  • Network Security: Guarding computer networks from intruders and malware.
  • Application Security: Keeping software and devices free from threats and flaws.
  • Cloud Security: Protecting data and systems stored on remote web servers.
  • Endpoint Security: Securing individual devices like phones, laptops, and smart tools

About the Department:

The Department of Cybersecurity offers a comprehensive and integrated program aimed at preparing students to address the increasing challenges in this vital field. The program focuses on developing technical skills in data protection, securing systems and networks, digital forensics, detecting and mitigating security threats, as well as risk management. It also applies artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques in classifying and monitoring cyberattacks. The program provides strategic insights and advanced practical skills that enable graduates to contribute effectively to enhancing cybersecurity and protecting information across various business sectors.

Vision

Academic and research leadership in cybersecurity to support innovation and serve the community.

Mission

Offering distinguished academic programs to train specialized professionals in cybersecurity, promoting research and innovation, and actively contributing to the protection of the digital community and the support of knowledge-based economies.
